Annotation Instructions for Cracks
Objective

Highlight all visible cracks in the image for computer processing. Annotations must be created as masks that cover the visible crack area as accurately as possible.

Masking Requirements
Accuracy: high precision. Follow the visible crack width as closely as possible.
Do not make the mask significantly wider than the actual crack.
Include thin branches that clearly belong to the crack.
Small gaps may be ignored if the crack is not visibly continuous.
Do not annotate shadows, stains, texture, scratches, pores, or edges unless they are clearly part of a crack.
If several cracks are connected, they may be annotated as one continuous mask.
If cracks are clearly separate, annotate them separately.
Annotation Classes
Crack
All visible crack regions.
Include the full visible width of the crack.
Include connected branches.
Only annotate areas where a crack can be visually identified with reasonable confidence.
Background / Non-crack
Everything that is not annotated as crack is considered background.
No separate background mask needs to be drawn.

So in that screenshot, “Annotation Instructions” is basically a guide for the person doing the labeling. It does not itself tell SAM what a crack looks like.
